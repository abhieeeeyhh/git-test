What do I need to book?

A valid driver's license held for at least two years, a credit card in the driver's name, and a minimum age of 23. City cars start at 21. Everything is verified online before pick-up, so there's no paperwork at the door.

Is insurance included in the price?

Yes. Every rental includes collision damage waiver, theft protection, third-party liability and 24/7 roadside assistance as standard. A zero-excess upgrade is available at checkout if you'd like even more peace of mind.

Can you deliver the car to me?

We deliver to homes, offices, hotels and airports across 40+ cities. Choose your address and an exact time at checkout — the first 25 km of delivery are on us, and the handover takes about four minutes.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before pick-up for a full refund. Inside 24 hours, one day's rental is retained. If your flight is delayed or plans change, call us — we'll always find a fair way forward.
