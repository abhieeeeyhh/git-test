Our story

We started with a simple annoyance.

Renting a car used to mean reservation paperwork, a queue at the counter, keys handed over with a lecture, and a hope that nothing on the car was already damaged. It shouldn't work that way.

VELOCE is built around the opposite idea: the car is the only part you should notice. We keep every vehicle immaculate, price everything in the open, put a real human on the line around the clock, and meet you at your door with keys in hand. When you're done, it comes back to us, washed and checked, ready for the next driver.

The parts that usually waste your time — paperwork, counters, surprises at the kerb — are exactly the parts we removed.

Everything else stays: a spotless car, an honest price, a real person whenever you need one.
